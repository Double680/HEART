Given a question and a hierarchical table with its instruction and headers:
Question: <QUESTION> 
Table Instruction: <INSTRUCTION> 
Table Headers: 
<HEADERS>
Filter out IDs of question-irrelevant rows and columns for each table to simplify the context. 
- The headers of filtered rows and columns should not include useful attributes for answering the question.
- The output should be in following format within <answer> </answer> tags, where the filtered row IDs should be within <rows> </rows> tags and the filtered column IDs should be within <columns> </columns> tags:
<answer>
<rows>[ROW_1] | [ROW_2] | ...</rows>
<columns>[COL_1] | [COL_2] | ...</columns>
</answer>
where all [ROW_1], [ROW_2], ... and [COL_1], [COL_2], ... should be integer IDs, seperated by ' | '. 
For example, the following output are legal:
<answer>
<rows>3 | 6 | 7</rows>
<columns>2 | 5 | 8 | 10</columns>
</answer>
